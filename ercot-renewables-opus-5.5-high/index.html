About the data

  • Source: ERCOT Fuel Mix Report workbooks (IntGenbyFuel), 15-minute settlement generation summed to months.
  • Covers generation settled in the ERCOT market, roughly 90% of Texas load. Rooftop solar and generation consumed on site by industrial plants are not included.
  • ERCOT reported all natural gas as one fuel through 2008, then split out combined-cycle plants. Tooltips show the split where it exists.
  • "Other" before 2011 included biomass and small solar. Since about 2021 it is mostly battery discharge. Battery charging (ERCOT's WSL, negative) is left out of the shares and shown in the table.
  • Hydro and biomass are combined because each is under 0.5% of generation.
  • The time-of-day charts average ERCOT's 15-minute intervals (interval ending, Central prevailing time) over every day in the period, from 2022 on. Demand is total settled generation minus battery charging. Batteries are ERCOT's "Other" (mostly battery discharge) plus WSL charging. Seasons are calendar-year months: winter is Jan, Feb and Dec of the same year. A year is shown only when every month of the period has data.
  • Rebuild the data with python3 build_data.py.

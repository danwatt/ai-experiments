Road trip planner: screen designs

Seven screens for the planner described in PLAN.md, all set in Colorado, the development region: a one-day drive from Denver to Durango, a five-day sightseeing loop with a through point, a timed stop and zones, picking a stop, drawing zones, editing on the map, settings, and exports. The look follows the GeoTagger design system: dark, dense, system font, one blue accent, emoji icons always paired with a word. Places, times, counts and mile figures are illustrative; the real ones will come from Valhalla and OpenStreetMap.

Yellow numbered dots are design notes, not part of the app. Each screen lists them underneath.

Colorado loop: five days with a through point, a timed stop and zones

A → B → C → D → E. B (Trail Ridge Road) only shapes the route, C (Durango) is two nights, D (Great Sand Dunes) is a three-hour stop, and E brings the loop back to Denver. Four zones are on: one to avoid, one to drive through without stopping, one to skip for meals, one preferred for stops. Day 1 is selected.

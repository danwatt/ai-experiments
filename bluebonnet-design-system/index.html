Bluebonnet

Texas wildflower colors for calm, legible interfaces. Bluebonnet blue for everything that matters; paintbrush, firewheel and winecup scattered through it the way they grow along a roadside in April.

Ennis Bluebonnet Trails

The Ennis Garden Club maps more than 40 miles of roadside trails each April. Peak bloom usually lands in the second or third week; check the field report before you drive, and visited links turn winecup once you have read them.

Coordinates for the downtown trailhead: 32.3293° N, 96.6253° W.

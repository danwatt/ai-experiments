Geometry explainer

Simplify the borders, not the shapes

What a topology-preserving simplifier does that a plain one doesn't. About 3 minutes, with narration.

 

0:00 / 0:00

    What the animation computes

    Nothing here is a canned drawing. The map is generated from a seed and stored as 9 arcs between 6 nodes. The left-hand results come from running Douglas–Peucker on each county's ring by itself. The right-hand results simplify each arc once, with its end nodes fixed, and refuse any shortcut that would cross another line or sweep over a point it must stay clear of.

    In practice

    • ST_Simplify: one geometry at a time, no validity checks.
    • ST_SimplifyPreserveTopology: keeps each geometry valid, but each row is simplified alone.
    • ST_CoverageSimplify (PostGIS 3.4+), mapshaper, TopoJSON: simplify shared edges once, so neighbors stay matched.

    Rebuilding

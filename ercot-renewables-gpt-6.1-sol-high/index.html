From the source

What counts as renewable?

Wind, solar, hydro, and separately reported biomass. Nuclear is a separate, low-carbon source. Natural gas combines ERCOT’s Gas and Gas-CC categories.

Generation, not capacity

Values show electricity actually generated, in GWh in the download and TWh on the charts. Shares use total generation excluding signed WSL (storage charging load). Storage is shown separately in the month detail.

A few things to know

ERCOT covers most, but not all, of Texas. This is a saved snapshot, not a live feed. Early reports do not separate every fuel; unreported solar and biomass are shown as zero and may be included in Other. Initial settlements can be revised.

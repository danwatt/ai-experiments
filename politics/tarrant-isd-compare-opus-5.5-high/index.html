Federal money by program

School meals and the SNAP connection

USDA pays districts a set amount for every meal served, at a free, reduced-price or paid rate. Under the Community Eligibility Provision (CEP), a school serves everyone free and claims a share of meals at the free rate equal to its identified-student percentage (ISP) × 1.6. The ISP is mostly students matched to SNAP. Districts not on CEP claim students who qualify through SNAP matching or a family application. Either way, fewer SNAP households means fewer meals reimbursed at the free rate.

Sources and method

  • Revenue and spending FY2011–FY2025: Texas Education Agency, PEIMS district actual financial data (audited, every fund/function/object), for TEA district numbers 220901–220920. "All funds" means the General Fund, food service, federal and state/local grant funds, Debt Service and Capital Projects. Internal-service and fiduciary funds are left out so money isn't counted twice, and bond proceeds, refundings and transfers between funds aren't revenue. Charter schools in the county aren't included.
  • Federal categories follow TEA fund codes: 211/276 Title I; 224–226 IDEA-B; 266 (FY2020+), 277, 279, 281–285 COVID relief; 266 (FY2011) and 283–287 (FY2011–13) the 2009 stimulus and Education Jobs; General Fund objects 5931/5932 Medicaid. USDA child nutrition is federal revenue in the food-service fund, which districts book as enterprise fund 701, special revenue fund 240, General Fund-group fund 101 (Everman) or summer-feeding fund 242 (pandemic meals in Arlington and Hurst-Euless-Bedford).
  • School meals: Texas Department of Agriculture School Nutrition Programs meal reimbursement open data, program years 2015-16 to 2025-26, contracting entities 01017–01032. Carroll ISD doesn't take part in the National School Lunch Program, so it has no claims and almost no USDA revenue.
  • Enrollment: TEA Summarized PEIMS Actual Financial Data, fall survey enrollment.
  • Inflation: CPI-U (not seasonally adjusted) from FRED, averaged July–June to match the fiscal year.
  • Fiscal years run July 1 to June 30 and are named for the year they end, so FY2025 is the 2024-25 school year. District boundaries don't follow city or county lines: Northwest, Aledo, Burleson and others reach into Tarrant County but are based in neighboring counties and aren't shown.

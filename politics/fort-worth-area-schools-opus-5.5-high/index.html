Sources and method

  • School spending FY2011–FY2025: Texas Education Agency, PEIMS district actual financial data. Every expenditure carries an organization code; codes 001–699 are campuses and 7xx–999 are district-wide units. School spending here is everything coded to a campus in the General Fund, special revenue (federal and state grant) funds and food service. Debt service, construction and internal-service funds are left out. District-wide costs (central administration, most transportation and maintenance, technology, some special-education services) aren't assigned to schools, so school figures understate the full cost of educating a student; the panel shows the district-wide amount per student.
  • Funding source follows TEA fund codes: General Fund and state/local grants are "state & local"; 211/276 Title I; 224–226 IDEA special education; 266, 277, 279, 281–285 (FY2020+) COVID relief; other 2xx/3xx funds other federal; 701, 240, 101 and 242 child nutrition (food service, mostly paid by USDA). Function groups: instruction (11, 95); curriculum, libraries and campus leadership (12, 13, 21, 23); counseling, health and social work (31–33); food service (35); everything else.
  • School directory: NCES Common Core of Data, school years 2010-11 to 2023-24 via the Urban Institute Education Data API, and 2024-25 from NCES EDGE: coordinates, address, grades, enrollment, free/reduced-price eligible counts and teachers. Schools are linked to TEA campus numbers through the state school id. Per-student figures divide by that year's NCES enrollment.
  • School meals: Texas Department of Agriculture School Nutrition Programs meal reimbursement claims, one row per school per month, program years 2015-16 to 2025-26. Claim site ids match TEA campus numbers.
  • Map: base map is Esri's Light and Dark Gray Canvas (Esri, TomTom, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors and the GIS user community); drawn with Leaflet. District boundaries from the U.S. Census Bureau (TIGERweb unified school districts). The map needs an internet connection; everything else on the page works offline.
  • Fiscal years run July 1 to June 30 and are named for the year they end (FY2025 = 2024-25). Inflation: CPI-U, July–June averages, from FRED. Amounts stored rounded to $1,000. Rebuild with build/fetch.sh and build/build_data.py.

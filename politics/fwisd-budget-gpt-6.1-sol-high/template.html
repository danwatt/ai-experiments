Extraction & validation

Nutrition is taken from each annual financial report’s “Budget to Actual – Food Service Fund (GAAP Basis)” schedule, for fiscal years ended June 30, 2016–2025. Original, final, and actual columns are preserved separately. Federal nutrition revenue is the sum of breakfast, lunch, USDA commodities, snacks, and separately stated other federal revenue.

Governmental federal revenue comes from each year’s financial statement, Exhibit C-3, code 5900, including its continuation page. It excludes the enterprise Food Service Fund. Each annual statement is used instead of a later unaudited ten-year summary, which contains discrepancies (notably FY2018).

Text was extracted with layout preserved. The image-only FY2017 nutrition schedule and 2025–26 budget tables were read with OCR and visually checked. Nutrition revenue totals, operating expense totals, and net changes reconcile for all 30 year/basis combinations. Budget function and revenue totals reconcile, except for two $1 arithmetic differences printed in the 2023–24 book; the published totals are retained.

What this answers about food assistance

SNAP supports household grocery purchases. It does not appear here as a district school meal revenue line. Direct certification rules connect SNAP participation to eligibility for free school meals.

FWISD says all its schools participate in Community Eligibility Provision (CEP), with free breakfast and lunch for students in 2026–27. That describes current access, not a claim that every historical year used the same rules.

The series cannot isolate the effects of a specific SNAP policy, determine household SNAP benefits inside district boundaries, or measure meals served. Nominal dollar changes can reflect enrollment, participation, reimbursement rates, program mix, accounting, and timing. No inflation or per-student adjustment is applied.

The comparable detailed nutrition series stops at FY2025, the latest audited report found. Later budget books aggregate meal program revenue into “Other Resources”; no missing program detail is inferred or set to zero.

Hex 2048

2048 on a 19-cell hexagonal board, written in Game Boy (SM83) assembly and running here in a small JavaScript Game Boy emulator.

How to play

Every move slides all tiles as far as they go in one of six directions. Two equal tiles that collide merge into their sum. A new 2 or 4 appears after every move. Reach 2048, and keep going until no move is left.

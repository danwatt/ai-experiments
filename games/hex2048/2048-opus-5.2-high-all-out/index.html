Shared by both

  • Board on its point. Flat-topped cells make the six slide directions straight up, straight down and four diagonals, which sit on Q W E / A S D.
  • Drag-follow. Tiles move with your finger until they reach where the move would put them, partners about to merge swell. Let go early to cancel.
  • Unlimited undo that never re-rolls. Tile spawns are seeded and saved with every step. Undo plays the move backwards: merged pairs split apart, spawns fade out.
  • Hint. An expectimax search with a time budget nudges the tiles toward its pick.
  • Three boards, optional stones. Mini (7 cells), Classic (19), Grand (37). Stones are fixed blocks that split lanes, laid out fresh each game, never in the centre, never sealing a cell in. Goals tuned by letting the bot play each combination.
  • Synth sound, two voices. Merges ring a pentatonic note per tile value: marimba on the tabletop, glass bells at night.

8% or 4%? The order of returns decides

Dave Ramsey's 8% rule, the 4% rule, and 150 years of S&P 500 history. About 5½ minutes.

    What the animation computes

    Every chart is simulated live in the page from the same yearly data as the interactive simulator: S&P 500 total returns, 10-year Treasury returns and CPI inflation for 1872–2025 (Shiller to 1927, Damodaran from 1928), plus Shiller's CAPE. Each saver puts away $10,000 a year in today's dollars for 30 years, retires, and withdraws a fixed share of the nest egg, raised with inflation, at the end of each year.

    Caveats

    • A historical simulation, not a forecast or financial advice.
    • No taxes, Social Security, or spending cuts unless a scene says so.
    • Fees scene: a 5.75% front-end sales charge with typical breakpoints, plus 0.65% a year in expenses.

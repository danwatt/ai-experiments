Source & methodology · Phase 3

Data transcribed from the Nov. 4, 2026, 2:37 a.m. cumulative report linked via Tarrant County Elections — November 3 election reporting index, covering 87 party-labelled races (58 contested, 29 unopposed). This file embeds all displayed candidate counts, including third-party and write-in candidates. Nonpartisan city, school board, appraisal district and proposition contests are not included in this initial partisan-focused view. It is not a certified election return, and results may be revised.

Margin formula: (leading candidate votes − second-place candidate votes) ÷ all valid votes cast in that contest × 100. Reported shares are recalculated from the raw vote counts; displayed percentages may differ by 0.01 from subtracting rounded percentages. Voting-method tabs show a leader within that method, not necessarily the overall election winner. When a race is unopposed, no competitive margin is assigned. Absentee (“by mail”) and early in-person votes are kept separate; combining those makes the early overall vote if needed. Source report labels 0 of 3,079 precincts reporting despite publishing vote totals, so no completion percentage is inferred here. Because the same voter can vote in many contests, never sum vote counts across contests to estimate turnout. Candidate overperformance: the candidate’s share of D+R votes minus a countywide baseline (median of U.S. Senate and seven statewide executive races, or your selected comparison race). Federal/state legislative districts, commissioners, precinct and other geographically ambiguous offices are excluded from this benchmark analysis. Partisan butterfly: D-minus-R votes divided by all valid votes, distinct from the leader–runner-up winning margin. Votes to flip: minimum winner-to-runner-up vote switches for a strictly reversed lead, not a real-world election forecast.

Standalone file — no external libraries or internet connection needed to analyze the embedded data. Optional basemap tiles are loaded from Stadia Maps or OpenStreetMap. Export buttons download the currently filtered candidate/contest data, including the new calculated metrics. All six analyses are based on the embedded snapshot rather than a live feed. Open the file locally in a browser and keep it as a reusable base for future analyses. Live Clarity election summary ↗.

Precinct map: the official Texas Legislative Council Precincts26P (2026 primary) file is the boundary reference (Creative Commons Attribution). Verify boundaries against the 2026 general election precinct roster if subsequent changes were made. Imported vote records are separate data and do not alter the embedded election-night countywide report; the map compares only precinct candidate totals that the user supplies. No turnout or party vote share is inferred from a boundary or voter-history data. The precinct map supports compact shared-arc TopoJSON, ZIP shapefiles in Texas Centric Lambert Conformal, KML, KMZ, WGS84 GeoJSON, and local precinct candidate CSV/JSON. Once imported, use “Save offline HTML” to embed the added data. Precinct vote results may reflect a newer timestamp than the countywide snapshot; this map is not automatically reconciled with the older summary. The dashboard has no external JavaScript dependencies. Optional basemap tiles require internet access; select None (offline) to disable them.

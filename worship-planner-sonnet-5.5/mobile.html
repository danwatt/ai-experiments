SelahMobile companion

Plan on the go. Lead with confidence.

The phone app carries everything about a hymn except the sheet-music slides: key, start pitch, meter, hymnal numbers, scripture, topics, and how recently the church sang it. Slides stay on the desktop, where they’re built and presented.

  • Plan — review and lightly edit the order of service.
  • Hymns — search the metadata database; add to a slot.
  • Live — song-leader view with start pitch and hymn number.
  • Me — your assignments; accept or decline.

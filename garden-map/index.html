    Month by month

    Fall is the best planting season here: roots grow through the mild winter, so new plants handle their first summer better. Spring planting works too, but plan on watering more.

    • Bars show flowers only. The magnolias are evergreen and the grasses and Mexican heather hold their foliage most of the year.
    • The mandevilla bar ends when the pots come inside for the winter, usually late October.
    • A hot, dry August slows many of these down; a deep soak every week or so keeps them going.
